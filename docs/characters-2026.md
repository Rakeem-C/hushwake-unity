# Character design pass, October 11, 2026

Thirteen locally authored finished prefabs retain the existing cast's clothing palettes, working equipment and limb hierarchies. Continuous 96-sided facial surfaces blend cheek, jaw, nose and lip planes, with per-character jaw/nose variation, subtle skin pigmentation, facial contours and shaped ears. Layered coiffure shells, swept fine locks, face-framing tresses, knots/braids and distinct hair palettes replace retired source hair. Fine undyed fabric preserves the original dyes without exaggerated checker relief. Curved edge interpolation refines hands, sleeves, boots and cloth. Small enamel/brass crests and cuff fasteners finish the costumes.

Runtime expressions preserve face, eye and inactive eyelid pivots through material batching. Brief staggered blinks and restrained head movements follow the character; hair is attached to the face. Gameplay, dialogue, equipment and movement authority remain with the existing game. Wheel zoom now reaches 4.5m for closer character views.

The editor authoring/portrait process was iterated eight times. Close renders caught and corrected mirrored facial orientation, a misplaced crest, fractional-power endpoint NaNs, tube winding, coarse hair layers and excessive fabric relief. Retired source geometry must remain invisible after batching. All generated source assets are local to this checkout; the original source remains unchanged. No Nintendo or third-party character models were imported.

Static checks: 547 gameplay assertions and 596 art assertions passed. Character guards include applied skin texture, source-eye-plane handedness, finite vertices, bounded triangle counts, resting lids, retained animated face pivots, retained shoulder/elbow/knee geometry and blink closure. Studio portraits are diagnostic renders, not proof of browser animation or universal performance. Fresh Web proof and release identity will be appended after testing.
